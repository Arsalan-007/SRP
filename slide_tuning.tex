\begin{frame}{Question 5: What Is Our Architecture's Ceiling?}
    \small
    \textbf{Test:} every benchmark gets its own Optuna search (40--120 trials, TPE with median
    pruning), over a \textbf{widened} space --- up to 512 hidden units, 256 embedding dimensions,
    depth 3 --- with TabM-style weight sharing as one more searchable choice.
    Best configuration re-trained on \textbf{5 seeds}.
    \vspace{0.05cm}
    \begin{table}
        \centering
        \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.08}
        \scriptsize
        \begin{tabular}{l | c | c | c | c | c}
            \rowcolor{PrimaryColor} \color{white}\textbf{Dataset} & \color{white}\textbf{Untuned v4} & \color{white}\textbf{Tuned} & \color{white}\textbf{Change} & \color{white}\textbf{Best published NN} & \color{white}\textbf{XGBoost} \\
            California Housing (RMSE$\downarrow$) & 0.5404 & \textbf{0.4634} & \cellcolor{WinGreen}$+14.25\%$ & 0.459 \,{\scriptsize FT-T} & 0.431 \\
            \rowcolor{LightGrey} Adult (accuracy$\uparrow$) & 0.8589 & \textbf{0.8637} & \cellcolor{WinGreen}$+0.56\%$ & 0.859 \,{\scriptsize FT-T} & 0.8739 \\
            Jannis (accuracy$\uparrow$) & 0.7195 & \textbf{0.7259} & \cellcolor{WinGreen}$+0.90\%$ & 0.732 \,{\scriptsize FT-T} & 0.7233 \\
            \rowcolor{LightGrey} Higgs Small (accuracy$\uparrow$) & 0.7215 & \textbf{0.7239} & \cellcolor{WinGreen}$+0.33\%$ & 0.728 \,{\scriptsize MLP-PLR} & 0.7255 \\
            YearPredictionMSD (RMSE$\downarrow$) & 8.8293 & 8.8655 & \cellcolor{LossRed}$-0.41\%$ & 8.784 \,{\scriptsize NODE} & 8.819 \\
            \rowcolor{LightGrey} Covertype (accuracy$\uparrow$) & 0.9324 & \textbf{0.9686} & \cellcolor{WinGreen}$+3.88\%$ & 0.964 \,{\scriptsize ResNet} & 0.9689 \\
            Facebook Comments (RMSE$\downarrow$) & 5.9443 & \textbf{5.8053} & \cellcolor{WinGreen}$+2.34\%$ & 5.525 \,{\scriptsize MLP-PLR} & 5.383 \\
            \rowcolor{LightGrey} Santander (accuracy$\uparrow$) & 0.9147 & \textbf{0.9239} & \cellcolor{WinGreen}$+1.00\%$ & 0.924 \,{\scriptsize MLP-PLR} & 0.9235 \\
        \end{tabular}
    \end{table}
    \vspace{-0.1cm}
    \textbf{Result:} tuning improves \textbf{7 of 8}. \textbf{Covertype} reaches $0.9686$ ---
    above the best published neural network ($0.964$) and within $0.0003$ of tuned XGBoost.
    \textbf{Jannis} ($0.7259$) and \textbf{Santander} ($0.9239$) also beat tuned XGBoost.\\[0.08cm]
    \tiny Only YearPrediction still regresses. An earlier version of this search ranked trials
    under a shorter epoch budget than the final re-train, which cost accuracy on Jannis and Higgs;
    with search and final matched at 200 epochs both now improve. YearPrediction reproduced its
    previous result exactly, so its regression is real and not a budget artefact.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What the Search Chose --- and What It Settled}
    \small
    \begin{table}
        \centering
        \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.08}
        \scriptsize
        \begin{tabular}{l | c | c | c | c | c}
            \rowcolor{PrimaryColor} \color{white}\textbf{Dataset} & \color{white}\textbf{Experts $K$} & \color{white}\textbf{Width} & \color{white}\textbf{Weights} & \color{white}\textbf{Embeddings} & \color{white}\textbf{Parameters} \\
            California Housing & 48 & 74 & shared & linear & 206{,}033 \\
            \rowcolor{LightGrey} Adult & 9 & 49 & shared & PLR & 245{,}316 \\
            Jannis & 14 & 27 & shared & none & 33{,}544 \\
            \rowcolor{LightGrey} Higgs Small & 29 & 166 & shared & none & 104{,}601 \\
            YearPredictionMSD & 31 & 108 & independent & none & 1{,}575{,}931 \\
            \rowcolor{LightGrey} Covertype & 9 & 361 & shared & PLR & 681{,}530 \\
            Facebook Comments & 6 & 287 & shared & linear & 710{,}089 \\
            \rowcolor{LightGrey} Santander & 25 & 8 & independent & PLR & 296{,}077 \\
        \end{tabular}
    \end{table}
    \vspace{-0.05cm}
    \begin{block}{Few wide experts or many narrow ones --- the data decides}
        Optuna chose TabM-style \textbf{sharing on 6 of 8} datasets, and the chosen shape varies by
        two orders of magnitude: $48$ experts of width $74$ on California Housing against
        $9$ of width $361$ on Covertype. Covertype's search was \textbf{still improving at its
        final trial}, so $0.9686$ is a lower bound, not a ceiling.
    \end{block}
    \vspace{-0.1cm}
    \tiny Embeddings were also searched: PLR won on 3 of 8 datasets, plain linear on 2, and none at all on 3 ---
    reproducing our earlier finding that PLR hurts on YearPrediction. Caveat: the tuned Covertype configuration
    changes sharing \emph{and} width together, so this search does not separate the two.
\end{frame}
